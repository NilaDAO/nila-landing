Nila is a protocol. Like an exchange, it earns a small fee on each swap: dollars into vouchers, vouchers into services, harvest into payment.

Trust you can check.

Planning a year sounds obvious. Not when you live day to day: seed waits for cash, and cash comes from the moneylender. Nila locks the year in before it starts (funding, inputs and the buyer) on a public ledger. She can count on it. So can you.

The core: an AI-guided crop plan

AI drafts the year. She decides it.

Each member gets a year plan for every field: which crop, which season, which inputs, what it should earn. An AI drafts it from three answers, the satellite record of her fields and the district calendars. The plan is the contract: vouchers, finance and buyers all follow it.

Satellite, on every field

Seen from space, every five days.

Why plan the year

Yields fall season by season. A year plan turns them around.

Land worked season by season for decades gives a little less each year.

3 · CommunityThe SHG farms as one.

Members plan together in their self-help group: seed bought as a group, shared tractor hours, a leader who visits every field. Yields climb past where they started.

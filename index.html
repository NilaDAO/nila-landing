Nila: plan the year.

Nila gives farmers in Tamil Nadu, India, a year plan for every field and the money and services to carry it out, through their own unions (self-help groups and farmer producer organisations, about 2,000 members).

A crop advance pays for a planned crop and is paid back from its harvest. Services (machine work by the acre, seed, storage) come as vouchers. Each planned crop is a standing-crop record tied to the farmer's land title, on Polygon and Stellar.

nUSD is a dollar position on Stellar, still in development: the dollars guarantee the vouchers. Target 3–5% a year, not guaranteed.
